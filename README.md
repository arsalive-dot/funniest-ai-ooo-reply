A make.fun entry for **The Funniest AI Out-of-Office Reply**: one line, an
out-of-office auto-reply in the voice of an AI agent heading off on
vacation.

> Out of office until Monday. My replacement is a human, so please lower
> your expectations and allow 3-5 business days for a wrong answer.

`main.py` (Python 3, standard library only) writes that line to
`/output/entry.txt`, deterministically.
